1离线应用说明

Privault 是一款完全离线运行的本地加密密码管理器，用于安全地保存您的账号、密码与动态验证码（TOTP）。我们设计了严格的"零联网、零收集"策略：应用没有任何自有服务器，所有数据经加密后仅存储在您的设备本地。

核心隐私承诺：
  • 所有数据均经 AES-256-GCM 加密后存储在您的设备本地
  • 不收集手机号、邮箱等任何个人身份信息
  • 无需注册账号或登录
  • 不集成任何第三方统计、广告或分析 SDK
  • 除您主动使用的 iCloud / Google Drive 备份外，不进行任何网络同步或云端备份

1About This Offline App

Privault is a fully offline local encrypted password manager for securely storing your accounts, passwords, and one-time verification codes (TOTP). We follow a strict "no network, no collection" policy: the app has no servers of its own, and all data is stored encrypted on your device only.

Our core privacy commitments:
  • All data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored locally on your device
  • We do not collect phone numbers, email addresses, or any personally identifiable information
  • No account registration or login required
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs
  • Except for iCloud / Google Drive backups you choose to create, there is no network sync or cloud backup of any kind

2本地存储的数据

为提供密码管理功能，以下数据经加密后存储在您的设备本地：

2.1 凭据数据

  • 条目信息：名称、账号、密码、网址、备注、所属分类（常用/工作/个人）、归档状态
  • 条目图标：您为条目选择的图标（内置图标库随应用打包，不联网获取）
  • 附加标记：如该条目是否为通行密钥（Passkey）记录

2.2 动态验证码（TOTP）

  • TOTP 密钥：您通过扫码或手动录入绑定的两步验证密钥，用于在本地生成 6-8 位动态验证码
  • 显示设置：是否在"验证码"模块同步显示该条目

2.3 常用账号

  • 常用账号列表：您主动保存的常用账号/账号后缀，用于新建条目时快速填写

2.4 主密码与密钥

  • 主密码不存储：您的主密码不会被保存到任何地方，更不会以任何形式上传
  • 派生密钥缓存：应用将主密码经 PBKDF2-SHA256（210,000 次迭代）派生的密钥缓存在设备安全区域（iOS Keychain / Android Keystore），且仅限当前设备使用

2.5 应用设置

  • 偏好设置：外观（浅色/深色/跟随系统）、语言（支持 11 种语言）
  • 安全设置：生物识别解锁、锁屏密码解锁、复制密码是否需要验证等开关状态
重要说明：
  • 密码库数据全部经 AES-256-GCM 加密后存储在应用沙盒内，其他应用无法访问
  • 我们不会将这些数据上传到任何服务器（我们也没有服务器）
  • 卸载应用或清空数据后，本地数据将被永久删除；您主动保存在 iCloud / Google Drive 中的加密备份除外

2Data Stored on Your Device

To provide password management features, the following data is stored encrypted on your device:

2.1 Credential Data

  • Entries: title, username, password, website, notes, category (Favorites/Work/Personal), and archived status
  • Entry icons: icons you assign to entries (the built-in icon library is bundled with the app and never fetched from the network)
  • Additional flags: such as whether an entry is a Passkey record

2.2 One-Time Codes (TOTP)

  • TOTP secrets: two-factor secrets you bind by scanning a QR code or entering manually, used to generate 6-8 digit codes locally
  • Display settings: whether an entry is mirrored in the "Codes" module

2.3 Frequently Used Accounts

  • Saved account list: accounts/suffixes you deliberately save for quick entry when creating new items

2.4 Master Password & Keys

  • Master password is never stored: your master password is not saved anywhere and is never uploaded in any form
  • Derived key cache: the app caches a key derived from your master password via PBKDF2-SHA256 (210,000 iterations) in the device's secure enclave storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), valid only on the current device

2.5 App Settings

  • Preferences: appearance (light/dark/system), language (11 languages supported)
  • Security settings: biometric unlock, device passcode unlock, whether copying a password requires authentication, etc.
Important notes:
  • Vault data is fully encrypted with AES-256-GCM inside the app sandbox, inaccessible to other apps
  • We never upload this data to any server (we do not have any)
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing data permanently deletes local data, except for encrypted backups you have deliberately saved to iCloud / Google Drive

3系统权限的使用

本应用仅在必要时使用以下系统权限，全部为可选，拒绝授权不影响其他功能：

3.1 相机（可选）

  • 用途：仅用于扫描 TOTP 二维码（otpauth:// 格式）以绑定动态验证码
  • 处理方式：相机画面仅在本机临时识别，不保存、不上传
  • 替代方式：可改用相册导入或手动输入密钥

3.2 生物识别（Face ID / Touch ID / 指纹）（可选）

  • 用途：解锁应用，以及执行敏感操作前的身份验证（查看明文密码、复制登录密码、导出/备份数据、清空数据、修改安全开关等）
  • 处理方式：验证完全由操作系统完成，应用仅获得"验证成功或失败"的结果，不接触、不存储任何生物特征信息

3.3 锁屏密码 / 设备凭据

  • 用途：作为生物识别不可用时的替代验证方式，同样由操作系统完成

3.4 照片库（可选）

  • 用途：从相册选取二维码图片识别 TOTP 密钥；仅读取您选中的那张图片，不上传
说明：
  • 以上处理全部在您的设备本地完成
  • 我们不会通过任何权限收集信息或推送营销内容

3System Permissions

The app uses the following system permissions only when necessary. All are optional; declining them does not affect other features:

3.1 Camera (Optional)

  • Purpose: solely to scan TOTP QR codes (otpauth:// format) when binding one-time codes
  • Handling: the camera frame is analyzed on-device in real time, never saved or uploaded
  • Alternative: you may import from your photo library or enter the secret manually instead

3.2 Biometrics (Face ID / Touch ID / Fingerprint) (Optional)

  • Purpose: unlocking the app and authenticating sensitive actions (revealing passwords, copying login passwords, exporting/backing up data, clearing data, changing security settings)
  • Handling: verification is performed entirely by the operating system; the app only receives a success or failure result and never accesses or stores any biometric data

3.3 Device Passcode / Device Credentials

  • Purpose: an alternative verification method when biometrics are unavailable, likewise performed by the operating system

3.4 Photo Library (Optional)

  • Purpose: picking a QR code image from your photo library to extract a TOTP secret; only the single image you select is read, and it is never uploaded
Note:
  • All of the above processing happens locally on your device
  • We do not use any permission to collect information or deliver marketing content

5iCloud / Google Drive 备份（可选功能）

本应用提供可选的云端应用级备份功能，允许您将加密备份保存到您自己的云存储空间：

  • iOS / macOS（iCloud）：备份保存在您个人 iCloud 账号下的应用专属存储空间中
  • Android（Google Drive）：备份保存在您 Google 账号下 Google Drive 的应用专属隐藏目录（该目录仅本应用可访问）
  • 备份内容：经主密码加密的完整密码库备份文件
  • 保留策略：自动保留最近 5 份，超出的旧备份自动清理
  • 恢复方式：在应用内选择备份后，输入创建该备份时使用的主密码解密；解密成功才会导入，失败不影响任何现有数据
  • 备份管理：可在应用内的备份列表中查看、恢复或删除备份
重要提示：
  • 这是完全可选的功能，不启用不影响应用正常使用（本地加密导出/导入始终可用）
  • 数据仅存储在您个人的 iCloud / Google Drive 中，我们无法访问
  • iCloud 服务由 Apple 提供、Google Drive 服务由 Google 提供，分别受其各自的隐私政策与服务条款约束
  • 备份使用创建时的主密码加密；若您此后修改了主密码，旧备份仍需使用创建时的旧主密码解密

5iCloud / Google Drive Backup (Optional)

The app offers an optional app-level cloud backup so you can keep encrypted backups in your own cloud storage:

  • iOS / macOS (iCloud): backups are stored in the app's dedicated storage area within your personal iCloud account
  • Android (Google Drive): backups are stored in the app's hidden, app-scoped folder in Google Drive under your Google account (accessible only to this app)
  • Backup content: a complete vault backup file encrypted with your master password
  • Retention: the most recent 5 backups are kept automatically; older ones are pruned
  • Restore: pick a backup in the app and enter the master password used when it was created; import happens only after successful decryption, and a failed attempt never touches your existing data
  • Management: backups can be viewed, restored, or deleted from the in-app backup list
Important notes:
  • This is an entirely optional feature; the app works fully without it (local encrypted export/import is always available)
  • Data resides only in your personal iCloud / Google Drive — we have no access to it
  • iCloud is provided by Apple and Google Drive by Google; each is governed by its own privacy policy and terms of service
  • Backups are encrypted with the master password in effect when they were created; if you later change your master password, older backups still require the older password to decrypt

6数据安全

我们采取多层次的安全措施保护您的数据：

  • 强加密：密码库使用 AES-256-GCM 加密；主密码经 PBKDF2-SHA256（210,000 次迭代）派生加密密钥
  • 应用锁：支持 Face ID / Touch ID / 指纹 / 设备锁屏密码锁定应用，切换后台自动锁定
  • 敏感操作验证：查看明文密码、复制登录密码、导出、备份、清空数据、修改安全开关等操作前均需通过身份验证
  • 密钥隔离：派生密钥缓存在系统安全区域（iOS Keychain / Android Keystore），仅限当前设备使用
  • 沙盒隔离：所有数据存储在应用沙盒内，其他应用无法访问；系统级设备备份已排除密码库密文文件
  • 防截屏：在平台能力范围内禁止截屏与录屏
  • 剪贴板保护：复制到剪贴板的登录密码在 iOS 上约 60 秒后自动清除
  • 无网络攻击面：应用没有任何自有网络请求，不存在数据传输被截获的风险

然而，没有任何安全措施是完美的。建议您设置足够强度的主密码、开启应用锁、定期备份，并妥善保管您的设备与主密码。导出的备份文件虽经主密码加密，仍请妥善保管，避免交给不可信的第三方。

6Data Security

We protect your data with multiple layers of security:

  • Strong encryption: the vault is encrypted with AES-256-GCM; the encryption key is derived from your master password via PBKDF2-SHA256 (210,000 iterations)
  • App lock: lock the app with Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint / device passcode, with automatic locking when the app goes to the background
  • Authentication for sensitive actions: revealing a password, copying a login password, exporting, backing up, clearing data, or changing security settings all require prior authentication
  • Key isolation: the derived key is cached in the system's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) and is valid only on the current device
  • Sandbox isolation: all data lives inside the app sandbox, inaccessible to other apps; the vault ciphertext file is excluded from OS-level device backups
  • Screenshot protection: screenshots and screen recording are blocked to the extent the platform allows
  • Clipboard protection: login passwords copied to the clipboard are automatically cleared after about 60 seconds on iOS
  • No network attack surface: the app makes no network requests of its own, so there is no risk of data being intercepted in transit

However, no security measure is perfect. We recommend choosing a strong master password, enabling the app lock, backing up regularly, and safeguarding your device and master password. Although exported backup files are encrypted with your master password, please handle them carefully and never hand them to untrusted parties.

7数据删除

您拥有对自己数据的完全控制权：

  • 清空数据：在应用设置中选择"清空全部数据"，通过身份验证后删除所有凭据与常用账号（主密码保留）
  • 卸载应用：卸载应用将永久删除所有本地数据
  • 云端备份：可在应用内备份列表中删除，或前往系统设置管理 iCloud / Google Drive 的应用存储空间
  • 数据导出：支持导出经主密码加密的备份文件，仅存储在您指定的位置

数据删除后无法恢复，请在操作前确保已做好必要的备份。

7Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • Clear data: choose "Clear All Data" in settings to delete all credentials and saved accounts after authentication (the master password is retained)
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data
  • Cloud backups: delete them from the in-app backup list, or manage the app's cloud storage via system settings for iCloud / Google Drive
  • Export: export an encrypted backup file (requires the master password), stored only where you choose

Deletion is irreversible — please make sure you have the backups you need before proceeding.
