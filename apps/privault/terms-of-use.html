2Nature of the App

2.1 Features

Privault is a local encrypted password manager offering:

  • Vault: store and manage credentials such as usernames, passwords, websites, and notes, with categories, archiving, and entry icons
  • One-time codes (TOTP): generate two-factor codes locally, with QR-code binding and in-vault mirroring
  • Password generator: create random strong passwords by length and character-set rules
  • Security audit: local checks for weak and reused passwords and other risks
  • Frequently used accounts: save common accounts for quick entry
  • Encrypted backup: local encrypted export/import; optional app-level backup via iCloud (Google Drive on Android)

All data in the App is entered manually by you or generated locally by the App. The App does not connect to any website or service, nor does it verify or retrieve any of your credentials.
